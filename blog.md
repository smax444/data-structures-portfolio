# About Me

My name is Samuel Maxwell, and I am a student at UNC Charlotte studying data science. I am especially interested in sports analytics and learning how data can be used to answer questions about player performance, team success, and decision-making.

Before attending college, I served in the United States Marine Corps. My experience in the military taught me the importance of teamwork, communication, problem solving, and being able to perform under pressure. I want to bring those skills into my education and future career.

I became interested in data science because I enjoy sports and I have always been interested in understanding why teams succeed or fail. Data gives me a way to look beyond opinions and use evidence to answer questions.

Throughout this course, I hope to improve my Python programming, data visualization, data cleaning, and analytical skills. I also want to become more comfortable working with APIs and real-world datasets.

My long-term goal is to work in sports analytics, ideally in football. I am interested in areas such as player personnel, scouting, team performance, and using data to support football decisions.

This portfolio will document the projects and skills I develop throughout the semester.
